Clinical AI Tools

Advanced AI-powered tools tailored for hospice and palliative care settings. These solutions enhance clinical documentation, support decision-making, and ensure patients receive timely, appropriate care.

Acuity Tool

An intelligent patient prioritisation system built on IPOS (Integrated Palliative care Outcome Scale) and POI (Phase of Illness) and other metrics.

  • IPOS-based patient assessment and scoring
  • POI-driven care prioritization algorithms
  • Automated patient risk stratification
  • Visual priority dashboards and alerts
  • Ensures no patient is overlooked

Smart Letter Writing Beta

AI-powered letter generation that creates professional, personalized correspondence for patients, families, and healthcare providers. Designed to understand medical context and maintain the compassionate tone essential for hospice care.

  • Context-aware AI tuned to hospice care nuances
  • Professional templates for all communication types
  • Automatic patient data integration from SystmOne
  • Customizable tone and style preferences

Smart Speech to Text

AI-powered speech recognition and transcription that converts spoken words into text for documentation and communication within the hospice care environment.

  • Speed up note taking by up to four times
  • All speech data is processed within your Microsoft domain
  • Empower clinicians with accessibility needs
  • Works with all templates in SystmOne

Clinical Auditing

Automated clinical quality assurance system that continuously monitors patient records to identify missing data, medication discrepancies, and documentation issues. Supports regulatory compliance and comprehensive patient care.

  • Detection of missing patient data fields
  • Medication reconciliation and safety checks

  • Real-time alerts for incomplete documentation
  • Full audit trails and reporting

Automated SystmOne Task Reminders

Intelligent task management that automatically monitors SystmOne for overdue or approaching tasks, sending timely reminders to the right team members. Ensures critical care activities are not missed.

  • Smart task prioritization based on urgency and patient needs
  • Automated escalation for overdue critical tasks
  • Customizable reminder schedules
  • Team workload balancing and task redistribution
  • Task completion tracking and reporting
